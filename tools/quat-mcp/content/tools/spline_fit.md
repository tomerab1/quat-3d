Turn a drawn stroke into a clean spline, the way a player's drag becomes a wall or a path. You pass the stroke as world points in drawing order. The engine resamples it, removes hand jitter (`jitter_tolerance`) and fits smooth curves that stay within `fit_tolerance` metres of it. Sharp turns (`corner_cos`) stay sharp. A point marked `{"position": [...], "straight": true}` makes the segment from the previous point exactly straight. A stroke that ends within `close_radius` of its start becomes a closed loop.

The result is a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline (`output: "cubic_bezier"` returns the fitted Bézier pieces instead). Pass `entity` to write it there: the entity's spline is replaced, but its `network` and `id` are kept. Without `entity`, the spline is only returned, and you can pass it to `spline_set`. With `"ground": true`, the points are put on the terrain and the spline follows it.
