Whether the terrain is ready: `{present, entity, streaming, generating, tiles_loaded, tiles_wanted, anchor, tile_size, bounds: {min, max} | null}`. `generating` is true while tiles are still being generated or uploaded (a new terrain, a shape edit such as a new `seed` or `height`, or the camera moving into a new streaming area); `bounds` covers the tiles loaded so far. Call it after creating or reshaping a terrain and before placing objects on it or taking screenshots; while `generating` is true, ground heights may be missing and the viewport may show no terrain. Returns `{present: false}` when the scene has no terrain.
