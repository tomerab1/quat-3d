Capture the engine viewport as a PNG image so you can see the world. Returns the image plus its size. Use after building to check proportions, placement (floating or buried objects), colours and lighting, then fix and look again.

Pass `camera` to look from somewhere else without moving the user's view: `{"camera": {"position": [40, 25, 40], "look_at": [0, 2, 0]}}`, or `{"camera": {"frame": "Watchtower"}}` to fit an entity in view (keeping the viewport's heading, looking down at least 20 degrees). Omitted camera fields keep the viewport camera's values; `fov_deg`, `near` and `far` work as in `camera_set`. Prefer this over `camera_set` for verification shots (close-ups, the far side, overviews): the user may be looking at something, and their camera stays where they left it.

Limitation: the engine renders one frame from the requested camera, so the user's viewport shows that view for a single frame (a brief flicker), and auto-exposure keeps adapting to the viewport, so a shot whose brightness differs a lot from the viewport's may look slightly over- or under-exposed. Terrain streams around the viewport camera, so a shot far from it may show missing tiles; check `terrain_status`, or move the viewport camera there with `camera_set` first.
