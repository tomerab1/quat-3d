Workload counters of the running engine: the last frame's `counters` (`draws` submitted after batching, `instances`, `triangles` of the scene meshes, `vram_usage_mib` / `vram_budget_mib`), GPU memory per heap (`gpu_memory.heaps`: budget, usage, what the engine's allocator holds and uses, allocation count; `device_local` heaps are VRAM, or the unified memory of an integrated GPU) and entity counts by component (`entities`: meshes, skinned meshes, instanced meshes and their `instance_placements`, lights, rigid bodies, characters, nav agents, behaviour trees, scripts, particle emitters, audio sources).

Use it to explain a cost found with `profile_frame` / `profile_capture`: a GPU-bound frame with millions of `triangles` or thousands of `draws` points at geometry (merge entities into an `instanced_mesh`, lower polycounts); `usage` near `budget` means the GPU is out of memory and will stutter.
