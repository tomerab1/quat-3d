The PBR material factors an entity is drawn with (a primitive or a mesh/glTF part; not an instanced mesh). Returns `{id, name, source: "primitive"|"mesh", material: {base_color, emissive, metallic, roughness, occlusion_strength, normal_scale, alpha_cutoff, transmission, ior, attenuation_color, attenuation_distance, thickness, clearcoat, clearcoat_roughness}, overridden: [fields], textures: [slots], transparent, shared_by?}`.

`overridden` lists the factors tuned on this entity (the rest come from the primitive or the shared material). `textures` names the texture slots the material samples — a textured factor multiplies its texture (e.g. roughness scales a roughness map). `shared_by` is how many entities draw the same material asset; `material_set` changes only this entity.
