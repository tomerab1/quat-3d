The junction graph of every spline whose `network` field is the given number (set it with `spline_set`), seen from above. Endpoints within `snap_radius` metres join one node. An endpoint resting on another spline's middle splits that spline (a T), and splines are split where they cross.

Each node has an `id`, a `position`, a `kind` (`end`, `straight`, `bend`, `tee`, `cross` or `n_way`) and the `ends` of the edges that leave it, ordered counter-clockwise seen from above, each with its XZ `direction`. Each edge has an `id`, the `entity` and `spline_id` it comes from, its parameter range `t0`..`t1` on that spline, its `length`, and its `from` / `to` node ids. Ids are 16-digit hex strings. They derive from the splines' ids, so they stay the same when you reshape a spline without changing what it meets.
