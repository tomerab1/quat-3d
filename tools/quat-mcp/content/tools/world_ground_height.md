Terrain height (world y, metres) under world-space `[x, z]` points: `{heights: [y | null], generating}` in input order. null means no terrain tile is loaded there (outside the terrain, outside the streaming window, or still generating: then `generating` is true; wait with `terrain_status` and ask again). Errors with not_found when the scene has no terrain. Heights follow terrain moves made earlier in the same `scene_batch`. Use it to place objects on uneven ground (set position y = height + the distance from the object's origin to its bottom). At most 1024 points per call.
