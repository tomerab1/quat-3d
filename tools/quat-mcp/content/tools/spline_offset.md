A curve parallel to a spline in the ground plane, as world points. `distance` is metres to the right of travel (negative: left). `width_factor` adds that many half widths, so `1` traces the right edge of a path drawn with the spline's widths and `-1` its left edge. `spacing` sets the metres between points (default 0.25). By default (`trim`), parts that come closer to the curve than the offset distance are cut away, such as the loops inside tight bends.
