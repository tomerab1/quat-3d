Read a camera rig, the orbit / strategy camera on an entity. You get its targets (`focus`, `yaw_deg`, `pitch_deg`, `distance`), its limits and smoothing half-lives, its `bounds`, and `current`: where the damped camera is now (`focus`, `yaw_deg`, `pitch_deg`, `distance`, `eye`). If the entity has `camera_rig_input`, you also get the input actions that drive it. With no `entity`, the tool reads the rig on the active camera.
